================================================================
 RITUAL - a memecoin whose supply is a function of what the
 community does, and whose treasury runs explicit rules.
 track: NEAR Hackathon, Memecoins With Mechanics (bnty_0421)
================================================================

RULE SET (every number lives in src/state.js)
  quorum                    3 votes - one vote per address per epoch, a whale's vote capped at 1
  min stake                 1,000 tokens
  mint cap                  500,000 tokens per ritual, scaled by participation
  steward                   2.0% of every mint
  pool fee                  3.00% - collected in USDC, goes to the treasury
  treasury floor            10,000 tokens - the treasury mints itself up to it
  treasury ceiling          10,000 USDC - above it the treasury buys and burns tokens
  chant fee                 100 tokens per signer, burn 5,000 tokens per signer
  howl tax                  1.00% of every trade burned while a howl window is open

SCENARIO 1 - THE CHANT: the community performs a ritual, the supply reacts
  supply before            7,905,000
  eligible holders         9 (the whale abstains; the steward holds no tokens)
  chanters                 mara, ivo, sana, tomas, wren, kofi
  ritual id                rit_1_CHANT_251783c6
  votes                    6.00 of 3 needed -> FIRED
  participation            66.7% of eligible holders took part
  minted to the community  333,333 tokens (cap 500,000, scaled by participation)
  steward cut (2%)         6,667 tokens - disclosed, not hidden
  chant fees to treasury   600 tokens (100 per signer)
  RULE R1 liquidity floor  treasury minted 9,400 tokens up to its floor
  supply after             8,247,733  (+342,733 tokens)

SCENARIO 2 - THE STORM: usage funds the treasury, then a ritual makes it act
  supply before            7,905,000
  the whale trades 8 x 80,000 USDC into the pool (3% fee each)
  fees collected           19,200 USDC -> treasury USDC 19,200
  price moved              0.858551 USDC per token
  ...then the community chants again, and the treasury rules run on the fees
  RULE R1 liquidity floor  treasury minted 9,700 tokens up to its floor
  RULE R2 buyback          treasury spent 9,200 USDC of fees, bought and burned 7,855 tokens
  supply after             8,073,512  (+168,512 tokens)
  the mint and the buyback pull against each other - that is the point: the
  rules, not a marketing plan, decide how much of the token exists.

SCENARIO 3 - NO QUORUM: a ritual that nobody backs does nothing at all
  2 signers for BURN       votes 2.00 of 3 -> PENDING, nothing happened
  supply unchanged         7,905,000 (was 7,905,000 - the ritual did nothing)
  a third holder shows up later:
  votes                    3.00 of 3 -> FIRED
  burned                   15,000 tokens (5,000 per signer)
  RULE R1 liquidity floor  treasury minted 10,000 tokens up to its floor
  supply after             7,900,000  (-5,000 tokens)

SCENARIO 4 - THE HOWL: a ritual window burns a share of every trade
  window open              epoch 1 -> 2
  a 50,000 USDC trade      fee 1,500 USDC + howl tax 189 tokens burned
  RULE R1 liquidity floor  treasury minted 10,000 tokens up to its floor
  supply after             7,914,811  (+9,811 tokens)
