## 3. Honest Assessment: Onboarding, Receipts & Value
- **Onboarding & Day Pass vs. Free Tier:** x402 onboarding was frictionless—a single `GET /api/x402/day-pass` challenge and gasless EIP-3009 signature activated the pass in `<2s`. The most useful tools were `handover`/`handover_accept` and `live_price`. On the Day Pass, `prompt_shield` and `live_price` switched to `plan: day_pass` (`calls_remaining: unlimited` vs. `20/day` free), though `provibe_audit` noted the upstream AI review provider fell back to deterministic static analysis (`10/108` files sampled).
- **Perspective on Receipts (Rating: 4 / 5):** Receipts are genuinely useful for multi-agent handoffs and bounty/client audit trails. Verification changed how we assessed `prompt_shield`: inspecting `canonical_bytes` revealed that input arguments are not committed in `output`, explaining why two different inputs shared the same `output_hash`. Receipts establish post-execution byte integrity (`output_hash`) and metadata binding (`metadata_hash`), while provider truthfulness, upstream fallbacks, and issuer identity (`Signature: NOT CHECKED`) still require independent checking.
- **Overall Value & Top Improvement:** For `$0.99/24h` via gasless x402, we would buy a Day Pass again when delivering verifiable client/bounty workflows. **Single most valuable improvement:** Commit a canonical hash of the tool's **input arguments** (`input_hash`) inside `canonical_bytes` and sign receipts with a pinned Ed25519 key so verifiers can cryptographically bind *which exact input* produced the committed output.
