The HATSouth survey has discovered 73 exoplanets to date. The table below lists these planets, along with links to papers describing their discovery, as well as some of their fundamental properties. HATSouth discovery light curves for each planet are available. In most cases, data from follow-up photometric and spectroscopic campaigns are also provided.
